# Design Doc Review

## Verdict: approved

## Summary

The design doc is complete, internally consistent, and faithfully aligned with the spec across all 16 requirements and 17 acceptance criteria. Both scenarios (`coding-standards-php` and `coding-standards-inline-docs`) are fully specified with names, recommended prompts, and acceptance lists; the catalog promotion plan mirrors the established Themes/Block Editor/REST API/Common APIs precedent; all decisions are traced to spec requirements; failure modes are documented; and no out-of-scope work has crept in. The two prompts are verifiably tool-agnostic (no mention of Yoda, WPCS, PHPCS, PHPDoc, @param, @return, @since, brace style, or any function/hook name) while reliably eliciting the gradeable constructs (a recognize-or-fall-back conditional for Yoda gradeability; a multi-parameter, value-returning, documented function for PHPDoc gradeability). The acceptance lists satisfy the spec's enumeration requirements in full: Yoda conditions, brace style, spacing, and prefixed/snake_case naming for `coding-standards-php`; summary line, @param per parameter, @return, @since with three-digit version, and alignment for `coding-standards-inline-docs`. The design correctly delegates final prompt/acceptance wording to phase 4 while constraining the two load-bearing properties (tool-agnosticism and elicitation). No new harness steps, rubrics, or skill changes are introduced. The residual degenerate-shape risk (match/array lookup with no `if`; trivial parameter-less function) is identified and correctly accepted as an inherent judge-only risk. The design is implementable against the existing codebase and conventions without conflict.
